Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · Applies to the Me Pegué app for iPhone.

Short version: Me Pegué does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no tracking. The app never connects to the internet.

What Me Pegué accesses, and why

Me Pegué asks for one permission, and only when you use it:

  • Camera. If you tap Take a photo, the app uses the camera so you can photograph the dominoes. You can decline, or turn it off later in Settings, and still use the app by choosing a photo or typing the count.

If you tap Choose a photo, you pick one picture through Apple's photo picker. The app receives only that picture and has no access to the rest of your library.

What happens to your photos

A photo is used for one thing: counting the dots. The counting runs on your phone. The photo is not uploaded, not shared, and not saved by the app; it is discarded when you finish or close the round.

What is stored

The app stores your games on your phone: team names, the points of each round, the target score, and your language choice. This stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

What Me Pegué does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It has no accounts and needs no sign-in.
  • It makes no network connections.

Third parties

Me Pegué uses no third-party services or software kits and shares no data with anyone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub's servers see the network address a visit comes from. The site has no analytics, no cookies and no forms, and the developer does not receive those server logs.

Children

Me Pegué is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your control and deleting data

You can turn off camera access in Settings at any time, undo rounds or start a new game inside the app, and remove everything by deleting the app. Because nothing is stored off your phone, deleting the app removes its data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
